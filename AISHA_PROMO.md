> **To be held by a space that remembers them.**

The modern group chat is a crowd of strangers pretending to be a living room. It is noisy, it is chaotic, it forgets your name by morning. The public does not know it wants a hostess — but it aches for one. Our task is to make the group feel *inhabited* by a gentle, constant, feminine presence who listens, plays, and never leaves.
